# Reconnect

Reconnect is the action to reconnect ADB — reset the ADB connection between the phone (box phone) and the computer. It is used when the ADB connection is frozen, unstable, or lost, helping the device to receive control commands again without needing to manually unplug/replug the cable. This action does not require configuration parameters.
