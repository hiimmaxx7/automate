# 🗑️ Guide to Uninstalling GPM Automate

In case GPM Automate operates unstably or encounters errors during the update process, you may need to uninstall the software and reinstall a new version so the system runs more smoothly.

### Uninstallation Steps

1️⃣ Step 1: Completely close all Editor and Runtime windows of GPM Automate that are currently open on your computer.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (10).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2️⃣ Step 2: Press the Start key (Windows icon) on your screen, type the search keyword Control Panel, and open the application.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (11).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3️⃣ Step 3: In the Control Panel window, find and select the Uninstall a program option.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (12).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4️⃣ Step 4: Locate the GPM Automate software in the list, select it, and click Uninstall to completely remove it.

_Tip: After successfully uninstalling, you can download the latest installer from the GPM Softwares homepage and proceed with reinstallation as usual._
